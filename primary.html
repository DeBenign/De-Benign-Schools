Primary School Division

From Primary 1 through Primary 6, our pupils are engaged through structured academics, creative activities, and character-building programmes.

Curriculum

A Structured Path From P1 to P6

Our Primary curriculum builds steadily in depth and independence across three stages, equipping every child with strong foundations in Mathematics, English, Science, and Social Studies plus the character and confidence to thrive at the next level.

Lower Primary

Primary 1 – 2

  • Foundational literacy & numeracy
  • Phonics-based reading
  • Basic science & nature study
  • Handwriting & fine motor skills
Middle Primary

Primary 3 – 4

  • Expanded Mathematics & English
  • Basic science experiments
  • Social studies & civic values
  • Creative & performing arts
Upper Primary

Primary 5 – 6

  • Advanced Mathematics & English
  • Basic science, ICT & agriculture
  • Common Entrance exam preparation
  • Leadership & debate opportunities
