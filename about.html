Our Story

A School Built From the Ground Up, for the Community We Love

De-Benign Schools began with a simple but powerful conviction: that families in Omi-Adio and the wider deserved a private school of genuine quality, without having to travel far from home or stretch beyond their means.

What started as a small nursery has steadily grown, term after term, into a full nursery-to-secondary institution. Today we proudly run our Nursery & Montessori, Primary, and complete Junior Secondary (JSS 1 – 3) divisions for the 2026/2027 academic session with Senior Secondary on our long-term roadmap.

Every milestone we've reached has been earned through the trust of the families who chose us, and the dedication of teachers who show up each day determined to bring out the best in every pupil.

💡

Our Philosophy

Every child has the ability to succeed. We believe education should inspire curiosity, cultivate resilience, encourage independent thinking, and nurture the values that shape truly exceptional human beings long after they leave our classrooms.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the foremost nursery-to-secondary school in Omi-Adio. An institution synonymous with academic distinction, discipline, and holistic child development that makes our community proud.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver quality, affordable education from Montessori through Junior Secondary in a safe, inspiring environment. Building confident learners who grow into leaders with strong moral values and clear purpose.
